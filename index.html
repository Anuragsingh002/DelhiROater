Water Quality Awareness

Understanding Your Drinking Water TDS Level

TDS (Total Dissolved Solids) measures dissolved minerals, salts, and impurities in parts per million (PPM). Our technicians verify TDS in front of you during every visit.

50 – 150 PPM
Ideal Minerals
Optimal drinking water balance. Clean taste with healthy natural essential minerals preserved.
150 – 300 PPM
Good Quality
Acceptable for daily consumption. Water purifiers in this range are performing normally.
300 – 500 PPM
Fair / Hard Water
Elevated dissolved minerals. Cartridge inspection recommended to avoid premature membrane fouling.
500+ PPM
Needs RO Care
High hardness & heavy mineral content. Requires multi-stage reverse osmosis filtration to ensure health safety.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our doorstep service, compatibility, and transparent pricing.

How quickly can a technician visit my location?
We provide prompt same-day service bookings. In most metropolitan coverage zones, an experienced technician will reach your location within 2 to 4 hours of confirmation, or at your chosen convenient time slot.
Are your spare parts compatible with my water purifier model?
Yes. We carry high-grade, standard-specification compatible components (membranes, spun filters, carbon blocks, booster pumps, adapters) suitable for all standard multi-stage domestic and commercial RO systems.
Are there any hidden diagnosis or travel charges?
No, our pricing policy is completely transparent. The standard inspection fee is clear, and if any parts require replacement, the technician provides a clear quote before starting any work.
How do I know if my RO membrane or filters need changing?
Common signs include a noticeable change in water taste or smell, significantly slower water flow into the storage tank, continuous water drainage, or a rise in TDS readings. If your filters have not been replaced in 9-12 months, a preventive check is recommended.
Is Radhey Radhey RO Service an authorized brand center?
Radhey Radhey RO Service is an independent third-party service provider. We are not an official or authorized service center for any specific brand. We provide independent repair, maintenance, and compatible spare parts across multiple brands.
